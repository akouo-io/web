# Export

Export sends the structured output of a meeting — the action items, the notes,
the decisions you've captured — into the tools where your work actually happens.
You review what Akouo pulled out of a meeting, and with one action it becomes a
Jira ticket, a Linear issue, a GitHub issue, or a page in Notion.

## Where each thing goes

Akouo matches the shape of what it extracted to the right kind of destination,
so you're never forced to reshape your output to fit a tool:

- **Action items → your issue tracker.** An item with an owner and a due date
  becomes a ticket in Jira, an issue in Linear, or a GitHub issue — assignee and
  deadline carried across.
- **Notes and summaries → your docs.** A recap or a topic breakdown becomes a
  page in Notion, ready to share or file.

Whatever your template called these fields, Akouo knows an action item is an
action item. Rename it, reshape your template, use a different name next quarter
— export keeps working, because it reads the shape, not the label.

## You export decisions, not guesses

You can only export something you've touched. An action item Akouo surfaced is a
suggestion until you accept it, assign it, or check it off — and only then can
it leave for Jira. Unconfirmed AI detections never land in your backlog. What
reaches your tools is what you decided was real.

## One item, one ticket

Once you've filed an action item as a ticket, Akouo remembers the link. If you
re-run the meeting through a better model later, it won't file a duplicate — it
recognizes the item already has a ticket and carries the reference forward. The
connection to a real, already-filed issue is never lost to a re-extraction.

## One-way, on purpose

Export pushes out; it doesn't quietly pull back. Akouo sends your action item to
Jira and remembers where it went — but it stays a meeting-intelligence tool, not
a second task manager fighting your real one for the truth. The stored link
leaves room to sync status back someday, but that's a deliberate future step,
not an assumption baked in today.

## Your tools, your connections

You connect the tools you use — authorize Jira or Linear, pick the project or
board things should land in, and Akouo handles the rest. Every export is logged,
so there's always a record of what left Akouo and where it went.
